The world

A whole archipelago, painted in code

Every world grows from a seed: its coasts, cliffs, beaches, foam and shallows are painted pixel by pixel while you play, so the islands never look like a grid of tiles. Even the sound is synthesised in your browser.

Explore by sea

Launch scout ships and lift the fog for everyone. Every island you find gets a name on the shared chart of the archipelago.

Settle and grow

Villagers find work on their own: they build, fell trees, quarry, farm and forge tools. Ferry them across to settle new islands.

Trade routes

Each island keeps its own stockpile. Build docks and send cargo ships to bring the goods home to the treasury.

Pirates and plunder

Raiders strike at dusk. Arm your ships, send out patrol boats, loot the wrecks and dive for sunken treasure.

Night and storms

Days pass, windows light up after dark and storms roll across the sea. A lighthouse keeps watch through the night.

The Great Work

Gather rare goods from six far biomes to raise a monument in three stages, then read the chronicle of your expedition.

Tribes

Choose your people

The whole crew plays one tribe. It decides how your buildings and villagers look, which biome you call home, and one bonus.

Biomes

Ten biomes, near and far

Gentle islands lie close to home; the hellish and magical ones wait far out at sea, each with its own ground, plants, deposits and mood.

    Day and night

    Keep a light burning

    Dawn is warm, dusk golden and night deep blue with fireflies. After dark your lookouts see less, and that is when the raiders come.

    Co-op

    Bring your crew
